Philosophy

Why Pause?

Most tracking apps are designed to make you do more. Pause is designed to help you notice more. We believe self-awareness—not optimization—is the foundation of meaningful change.

The app was born from a simple idea: what if tracking your habits felt less like a performance review and more like a quiet conversation with yourself? No streaks to protect, no gamification to manipulate. Just an honest, clear record of your days.

We imagine people using Pause to check in with themselves—to notice that they sleep better when they walk in the morning, or that their mood dips when they skip meals. Small observations that, over time, become real self-knowledge.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026. Please review and customize this policy to match your actual data practices before publishing.

Data Collection

Pause collects only the data you explicitly enter into the app, including habits, biometric measurements, goals, and activity logs. We do not collect analytics, location data, or device identifiers.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and is associated with your authenticated account. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or provide your personal data to any third parties. Your data is only accessible to you through your authenticated account.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Authentication — Used to securely manage your account and sign-in credentials.
  • Cloud Firestore — Used to store your app data in your personal account.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.
